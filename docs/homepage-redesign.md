# Shelf Merch homepage redesign

The homepage retains the royal blue hero (#3155ff), white headline and yellow italic accent (#ffd84a). The new layout uses Arial body copy at 19px desktop and 18px mobile, consistent 48px section headings on desktop, and simple buttons without arrows. Catalog categories, customer logos, team solutions, production, recognition, planning, testimonials and resources are brought into the shared design pattern used across the updated marketing pages.

New hero asset: `apps/web/public/images/landing/home-hero-v2.webp`. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool and saved with its transparent alpha preserved.

Prompt: Premium photorealistic corporate merchandise kit cutout for Shelf Merch homepage hero. Landscape 3:2, actual transparent background. One beautifully made open rigid cobalt-blue presentation box with a warm coral-orange interior lid and subtle small geometric brand motifs, no words or logos. Inside neatly fitted: folded cream cotton tee, navy cloth notebook, brushed steel insulated bottle, small dark tech case, cream welcome card with simple cobalt and yellow geometric pattern. Natural three-quarter overhead product photography, tactile fabric and paper, realistic proportions, carefully arranged generous breathing room between items, premium rather than cluttered. All box edges and the entire open lid completely within frame with 12% clear margins. Soft directional studio lighting and very subtle contact shadow only. No floor, no enclosing rectangular image background, no extra panels, no plants leaves trees hands or people, no floating UI cards. The finished transparent composition will sit directly on a royal-blue website background; retain clean visible box edges.
