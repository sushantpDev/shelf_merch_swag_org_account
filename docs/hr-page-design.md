# HR page design

The HR page uses the established Arial / italic Georgia typography with its own royal blue people-focused hero, alternating feature sections, a custom milestone calendar illustration, horizontal workflow and expandable controls. The supplied Stadium screenshots informed the progression from hero to benefits and final CTA. Photography, color treatment, compositions, copy and UI graphics are original to Shelf Merch. Existing HR program content is retained from `resources-data.ts`. Sales, events and the Resources hub are unchanged.

Three original images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then converted to WebP without cropping or semantic editing. All prompts exclude plants, leaves and trees. No Resources hub images are reused.

## Saved assets and prompts

### hero

Saved asset: `apps/web/public/images/resources/hr/hr-welcome-hero.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: HR SaaS website hero banner photography, portrait 4:5 framing. Create a premium candid editorial workplace photograph: a young Indian woman in an elegant ivory knit top receiving a cream canvas company welcome tote from a friendly Indian female HR colleague in a cobalt shirt. Both smiling naturally, authentic relaxed expressions, no posing at camera. Waist-up or seated medium shot with both faces and hands entirely visible, comfortably framed, no heads cut off. A folded lavender sweatshirt peeks out of tote. Minimal contemporary office with plain warm off-white wall and one cobalt blue wall panel, light oak table, soft daylight, realistic skin texture, tasteful editorial photography with light film grain. Palette royal blue, cream, warm neutrals, subtle lavender. Tote has tiny dark blue text 'Shelf Merch'. No gift box, no notebooks, no water bottles, no studio product still life. No plants, trees, leaves, flowers, botanical art or vegetation anywhere. No floating UI, no interface, no captions, no borders, no watermarks. Focus on the genuine first-day welcome between people.
