01 / THE BASICS

About these terms

ShelfMerch is operated by Chitlu Innovations Private Limited. These draft Terms of Service address the ShelfMerch Slack integration, which connects a ShelfMerch company account to a Slack workspace.

The integration is provided by ShelfMerch. Slack is a separate service, and your use of Slack is subject to Slack’s own terms and workspace policies. These terms concern the integration; they do not set merchandise order, payment or delivery terms.

02 / THE CONNECTION

Useful updates, in your channel.

The integration posts ShelfMerch business notifications to a selected public Slack channel. Available notification preferences cover wallet funding, budget assignments, low-budget alerts, points distributions, kit assignments, order dispatches, delivery issues and shop launches.

Authorized users manage the connection, select a default channel, change notification preferences and send a connection test message from ShelfMerch. Notifications may include links back to ShelfMerch.

A channel for operational updates

The current integration does not provide Slack slash commands, read conversation history or import private messages.

03 / YOUR AUTHORIZATION

Connect with permission.

Only connect a ShelfMerch company account and Slack workspace you are authorized to manage. You must have the relevant ShelfMerch integration permissions and any approval required by your organization or Slack workspace.

Review Slack’s authorization screen before granting access. You are responsible for choosing an appropriate destination channel and notification settings, and for ensuring the people who can access that channel are entitled to see the shared information.

04 / RESPONSIBLE USE

Keep the connection appropriate.

Use the integration for authorized business activity. Do not use it to send unlawful, abusive or misleading content, expose information without permission, impersonate others, bypass access controls or interfere with ShelfMerch or Slack.

Protect your ShelfMerch account credentials and review who can administer integrations for your organization. Contact ShelfMerch if you suspect an unauthorized connection or disclosure.

05 / ACCESS & PROCESSING

What the connection uses.

The current OAuth flow requests these bot permissions:

channels:read

Read basic public-channel information so you can select a destination.

chat:write

Post test messages and ShelfMerch notifications as the app.

chat:write.public

Post to public channels, including channels the bot has not joined.

Connection information

ShelfMerch stores the Slack workspace ID and name, enterprise ID and name when supplied, bot user ID, installing Slack user ID, associated ShelfMerch user and company identifiers, selected channel ID and name, notification preferences, connection status, timestamps and error information. The bot access token is stored encrypted using application-managed encryption.

Information sent to Slack

Depending on the event and your settings, notifications can contain company or team names, staff or recipient names, purchase-order and order references, wallet and budget amounts, point totals, recipient counts, kit and product details, courier and tracking references, delivery issue notes, shop details and links to ShelfMerch. Review channel membership before enabling these updates.

Notification records

ShelfMerch records notification event identifiers, related resource and connection identifiers, send status, timestamps and error codes to track delivery attempts and prevent duplicate sends.

06 / PRIVACY

Shared with your team.

Read the ShelfMerch Privacy Policy alongside this document. Your organization is responsible for the permissions and notices needed to share employee, recipient and business information through its Slack workspace.

Information posted to a channel becomes available according to Slack’s channel access, sharing and retention settings. Disconnecting the integration does not remove messages already posted to Slack. For questions about retained ShelfMerch records or deletion requests, contact support@shelfmerch.com.

07 / CONNECTION CONTROL

You manage the connection.

Connect through ShelfMerch’s Slack integration settings and complete the Slack OAuth authorization flow. After connecting, select a public channel and review which notification categories are enabled.

An authorized integration administrator can disconnect Slack from ShelfMerch. The disconnect operation clears the locally stored bot token and selected channel settings and marks the connection disconnected. ShelfMerch also attempts to revoke the token through Slack; remote revocation can fail independently of local disconnection.

Future notification attempts require a connected workspace, a stored token, a selected channel and an enabled event preference. An attempt already in progress may still complete. Disconnecting does not delete connection metadata, notification records or your underlying ShelfMerch business records.

08 / SERVICE AVAILABILITY

Notifications need a working connection.

Delivery depends on Slack’s APIs, valid permissions, your selected channel and the availability of both services. Messages may be delayed or fail to arrive, including when access is revoked or a channel becomes unavailable.

Check ShelfMerch directly for the current status of budgets, orders and other activity. Slack notifications are a convenient update channel and should not be your only way to monitor time-sensitive business activity.
