In short

Shelf Merch is a platform by Chitlu Innovations Private Limited. We use personal data to operate corporate merchandise programmes, process customised orders and deliver products. We do not sell or rent personal data.

01

Who this policy covers

One notice for website visitors, programme users and recipients.

This Privacy Policy applies when you:

  • visit a Shelf Merch website or contact our team;
  • use a company-branded store, merchandise catalogue, wallet or recognition programme;
  • receive a gift link, redeem points or place an order;
  • approve, administer or report on a corporate merchandise programme; or
  • receive an item produced, packed or delivered by Shelf Merch.

“Shelf Merch”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Chitlu Innovations Private Limited.

02

Our role and your company

Privacy responsibilities depend on how the programme is set up.

For website enquiries, direct orders and services where we decide why and how personal data is used, Chitlu Innovations Private Limited is responsible for that processing.

For a company-sponsored store, wallet, gifting or recognition programme, your employer, client or sponsoring organisation may decide who participates, what information is provided, which rewards are available and what reporting is required. In that context, Shelf Merch may process information on the organisation’s instructions while also remaining responsible for information we use for our own operational, security, billing and legal purposes.

Questions about an organisation’s HR decisions, eligibility rules or internal records should be directed to that organisation.

03

Information we collect

The details needed to run the programme and complete the order.
Account and contact details

Name, work email, phone number, company, department, role and login or account identifiers.

Programme information

Eligibility, department, budget or wallet balance, points, rewards, milestones, preferences and programme activity.

Order and delivery details

Products, sizes, quantities, personalisation, recipient name, delivery address, contact details, order status and tracking information.

Content you provide

Logos, artwork, messages, recognition posts, gift notes, feedback, support requests and communications.

Transaction records

Quotation, purchase order, agreement, invoice, GST and payment-status information. Payment providers may separately process payment credentials.

Technical information

Standard device, browser, IP address, access time, page activity, security events and diagnostic logs when you use our digital services.

We may receive this information directly from you, from your organisation or programme administrator, from another authorised sender, or from providers that help operate the service.

Please avoid including sensitive or unnecessary personal information in free-text messages, gift notes, artwork or support requests.

04

How we use information

To operate, fulfil, support and protect the Shelf Merch experience.

We may use personal data to:

  • create and manage accounts, company stores, wallets, budgets, campaigns and programme access;
  • display approved catalogues, eligibility, credits, points and relevant programme information;
  • prepare artwork, customise products, process orders, pack items and arrange delivery;
  • send order confirmations, gift links, reminders, dispatch updates and service communications;
  • provide customer support and resolve product, delivery or account issues;
  • give authorised company administrators programme, redemption, spend, order and fulfilment reports;
  • maintain service reliability, prevent fraud, investigate misuse and protect accounts;
  • manage quotations, invoices, payments, accounting and contractual obligations;
  • improve products, workflows and user experience using aggregated or appropriately protected information; and
  • comply with law, respond to valid legal requests and establish or defend legal claims.

We may send promotional communication where permitted. You can use the unsubscribe option or contact us to stop receiving marketing. Essential service and order messages may continue where required to complete a transaction or operate your programme.

05

Why we process it

We use an appropriate legal basis for each purpose.

Depending on the situation and applicable law, we process personal data:

  • with your consent for a clear purpose;
  • for the purpose for which you voluntarily provided the information;
  • to provide a requested service, fulfil an order or perform a contract;
  • on the documented instructions of a sponsoring organisation;
  • to meet legal, tax, accounting, security or regulatory obligations; or
  • for another use permitted by applicable law.

Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully and may limit our ability to provide the relevant feature or service.

06

Company administrators

Your sponsoring organisation controls its programme.

Authorised administrators and managers may be able to view or manage information connected with their organisation’s programme, including user access, department allocations, wallet or points activity, approvals, orders, redemption, delivery status and programme reports.

Recognition messages may be visible to other programme participants depending on the programme settings. Do not post confidential, offensive or unnecessary personal information.

If you leave the organisation or become ineligible, the organisation may ask us to change or remove access. Records may still be retained where required for completed orders, accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

07

When we share data

Only where it supports the service or is legally required.

We may share relevant personal data with:

  • your sponsoring organisation and its authorised administrators or programme managers;
  • production and fulfilment providers involved in sourcing, customisation, quality control, packaging and delivery;
  • courier and logistics providers that need recipient and address information to deliver and track shipments;
  • technology and service providers that support hosting, communications, account operations, customer support, payments or security;
  • professional advisers, auditors or insurers where reasonably necessary;
  • government authorities, regulators or courts where disclosure is required or permitted by law; and
  • a successor organisation in connection with a genuine merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements.

We share only the information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose. Service providers are expected to use it only for authorised services and protect it appropriately. We do not sell or rent personal data.

08

Cookies and similar technology

Used carefully to keep digital services working.

Our websites and stores may use essential cookies or similar technologies for functions such as session continuity, security, preferences and authentication.

If optional analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced, we will identify them through an appropriate cookie notice or consent choice where required. You can also control cookies through your browser, although disabling essential cookies may affect how the service works.

09

International processing

Some service providers may operate outside India.

Personal data may be stored or processed in India or in another country where Shelf Merch or a relevant service provider operates. When information is processed outside India, we will take reasonable steps to use appropriate contractual and security safeguards and follow any transfer restrictions under applicable Indian law.

10

How long we retain data

Only for as long as there is a valid purpose.

Retention depends on the type of information and why it is needed. We may keep data while an account or programme is active, while an order is being fulfilled, for customer support, and for a reasonable period afterwards to maintain transaction, tax, accounting, warranty, security and dispute records.

When information is no longer required for a valid business or legal purpose, we will delete it, anonymise it or securely restrict further use. An organisation may have its own retention obligations for information it controls.

11

How we protect data

Reasonable safeguards across people, process and technology.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. These may include access controls, role-based permissions, secure service providers, monitoring, backups and staff or vendor confidentiality obligations, as appropriate to the service and risk.

No website, platform, transmission or storage system is completely secure. If you believe an account or personal data has been compromised, contact us promptly.

12

Slack Integration Data

Limited Slack information used for workspace notifications.

When you connect your Slack workspace with ShelfMerch, we collect and process limited Slack information required to provide Slack notification features.

Information collected:

  • Slack workspace ID
  • Slack workspace name
  • Slack user ID of the person installing the integration
  • Slack bot token required to send authorized notifications

How we use this information:

  • Connect your Slack workspace with ShelfMerch
  • Send ShelfMerch notifications to your selected Slack channels
  • Maintain your Slack integration settings

Security:

  • Slack authentication tokens are encrypted before storage.
  • We do not read or store private Slack conversations.
  • We only request permissions required for ShelfMerch notification functionality.

Disconnecting:

Users can disconnect Slack from their ShelfMerch account. Disconnecting removes the stored Slack connection credentials according to our data retention practices.

13

Your privacy rights

You can ask questions and exercise applicable legal rights.

Subject to applicable law and verification, you may ask us to:

  • provide information about the personal data we process and how it is used or shared;
  • correct, complete or update inaccurate personal data;
  • erase personal data that is no longer required, unless retention is permitted or required;
  • withdraw consent where processing depends on consent;
  • address a privacy complaint or grievance; and
  • support any nomination right available under applicable Indian data protection law.

Email our contact form with the subject “Privacy Request”. We may need information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. For data controlled by your employer or sponsoring organisation, we may refer the request to that organisation or assist it in responding.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. You should first give us an opportunity to resolve a privacy grievance before approaching the relevant authority where the law requires this.

14

Children’s personal data

Shelf Merch is designed primarily for companies and adult users.

Our platform is not directed to children. We do not knowingly ask a child to create an account or provide personal data without the consent or other authorisation required by applicable law. If you believe a child’s personal data was provided improperly, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

15

Updates and contact

We will update this notice when practices materially change.

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, providers or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted with a revised effective date. Where a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will provide additional notice or seek consent where required.
