Why we exist

Merch should feel personal.
Running it shouldn't feel painful.

For too long, branded merchandise has meant chasing quotations, fixing artwork, guessing sizes, carrying excess inventory and following up with multiple vendors.

We built Shelf Merch to replace that friction with one clear system—while keeping the craft, flexibility and human attention that make a gift worth giving.

Our model

One shelf.
Three engines.

Technology coordinates the program. Our production team makes it tangible. Our fulfillment team gets it exactly where it needs to go.

Our story

We did not start by
building another catalogue.

We started by taking responsibility for the entire merchandise journey.

2023

From content to something people could hold

Shelf Merch began in Hyderabad as a Merchandise-as-a-Service platform helping creators and brands design, sell and deliver products without inventory or upfront production.

NEXT

The same problem was everywhere inside companies

HR, marketing and admin teams were still juggling vendors for onboarding, events, gifting and recognition. We brought those workflows onto one company-controlled platform.

TODAY

An operating partner—not a marketplace middleman

Today, we connect branded stores, budgets and campaigns with in-house production, kitting, packaging and fulfillment. Our YouTube Shopping partnership remains part of our creator-commerce foundation—not the limit of what we build.

Built, not brokered

The platform sees the order.
Our floor makes it real.

We keep the steps that shape quality close: artwork checks, sampling, DTF printing, Tajima embroidery, personalisation, quality control and final packing.
